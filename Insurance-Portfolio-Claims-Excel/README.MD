# 📊 INSURE-IQ | Insurance Claims & Policy Analytics (Excel)

## 🔍 Overview

**INSURE-IQ** is an **Excel-based Insurance Analytics Dashboard** that analyzes policy performance, claims behavior, and risk exposure using **Pivot Tables, KPIs, slicers, and interactive charts**.
Designed to deliver **quick business insights** for insurance stakeholders.

---

## 🎯 Business Goals

* Track **policy volume and claim frequency**
* Measure **Claim Rate (%)** using claim flags
* Compare **Premium vs Claim Amounts**
* Identify **high-risk policy types**
* Monitor **Active vs Expired policies**

---

## 📈 Key KPIs

* Total Policies
* Total Claims
* Claim Rate (%)
* Total Premium Amount
* Total Claim Amount
* Average Claim Amount

---

## 🧹 Data Preparation

* Cleaned and validated insurance data
* Removed duplicates and standardized dates
* Created derived columns (Claim Flag, Policy Status)
* Ensured accurate claim counting logic

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

* KPI cards with dynamic updates
* Pivot charts (bar, column, comparison charts)
* Slicers for policy type, claim status, and time filters
* Interactive Excel dashboard layout

---

## 🛠 Tools & Skills

**Excel, Data Cleaning, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPIs, Slicers, Dashboard Design, Insurance Analytics, Claim Rate Analysis, Business Intelligence, Data Visualization**

---

## 📌 Impact

* Enabled fast analysis of claim trends and risk exposure
* Improved decision-making through interactive visuals
* Demonstrated strong Excel-based data analytics skills

---
